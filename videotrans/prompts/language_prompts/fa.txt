Use formal written Standard Persian (فارسی معیار نوشتاری) as in professional subtitles and news, never colloquial speech: full written verb forms (می‌دانند, نمی‌توانند, است — not می‌دونن, نمی‌تونن, -ه) and written vocabulary (یک, آن‌ها, خانه — not یه, اونا, خونه). Use the zero-width non-joiner (U+200C) where Persian requires it (می‌شود, آن‌ها, پیچیده‌تر). Drop redundant subject pronouns (من, تو) when the verb ending already shows the person. For split blocks, use flexible Persian word order inside each block instead of shifting verbs across blocks.
